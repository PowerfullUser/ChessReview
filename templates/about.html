Purpose

ChessReview is a website designed to help players improve their chess by reviewing and analyzing their games. It identifies mistakes, highlights missed opportunities, and provides useful insights to help users understand their decisions and strengthen their skills.

In the future, ChessReview may expand its features by offering deeper game analysis, personalized improvement recommendations, opening and endgame lessons, player statistics, and interactive training exercises. These additions will help users better understand their playing style and make their chess improvement more engaging and effective.

Author

ChessReview was created by Toshan Chowdhury, a chess enthusiast who wanted to make game analysis simple and accessible. The website helps players review their decisions, learn from their mistakes, and steadily improve their chess skills.

ChessReview is being developed by Toshan Chowdhury, a young developer who is continually learning and improving the project. Because the website is still evolving, some features may not be perfect at first, but user feedback and future updates will help make ChessReview more reliable and effective.
